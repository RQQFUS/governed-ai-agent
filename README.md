# Governed AI Agent

I'm a cybersecurity Leader studying AI governance. This repo shows both sides of one small AI agent: the agent itself, and the rules that govern it.

Most governance work lives in documents nobody can test. Here, every rule should map to a control you can see.

## What's here
- policy/ai-use-policy.md: what the agent can and can't do, and who's accountable
- screenshots/: proof the approval rule works (agent stopped and waited for my go)
- More coming: EU AI Act classification, NIST AI RMF risk assessment, guardrails mapped to policy

## The agent
My Claude setup (built on Anthropic's model) acts as my chief of staff. It manages my job search, study plan, email, calendar, documents and web research. It drafts and prepares. I decide and send.
