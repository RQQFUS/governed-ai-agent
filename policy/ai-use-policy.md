# AI Use Policy

Owner: Lonnie Colbert. I'm accountable for everything this agent does.

Purpose: Acts as my chief of staff for my job search, study plan, email, calendar, documents and web research.

Can touch: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and a web browser.

Can't touch: Passwords, payment or bank details, and government systems, including my Army email account.

Personal information: It knows some details about me and my family that I've shared so it can help with scheduling. It can't share them with anyone without my approval, and none of it goes in this repo.

Needs my approval first: Sending, submitting, posting, publishing, deleting or buying anything.

Logs: Every action is recorded in my Claude conversation history. Gmail's sent and drafts folders are a second record.

If something goes wrong: I stop the agent, fix the damage, and log what happened as an issue in this repo.

Review: Every 90 days. Next review: February 2027.

Roles: I'm the deployer of this agent and built its setup. Anthropic is the provider of the underlying model.
